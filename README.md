# אוטו תכל׳ס · Auto Tachles

**Find a discounted AutoTel car. Get going for less.**

Auto Tachles makes it easier to spot discounted AutoTel cars around Tel Aviv–Yafo on one simple map. The goal is simple: less searching, more saving on your next ride.

See cars with 20%, 30%, or 50% discounts, find one nearby, and get walking directions to it. Available in Hebrew and English.

An independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by AutoTel. Always confirm the discount and availability in the official AutoTel app, where you book your ride.
